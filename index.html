FAQ

Common questions about
setup, pricing, and how the AI works

How long does it take to launch the platform?
+
Up to 5 business days — from filling out the brief to the first live conversations. This includes AI agent setup, CRM integration, and simulation.
Which CRMs are supported?
+
Bitrix24 and AmoCRM natively via API. For other systems — Custom API and webhooks. If you have a proprietary CRM, we'll prepare a custom integration based on an agreed technical specification.
Can I test the platform before paying?
+
Yes. Before launch we conduct a conversation simulation — a test environment where you see exactly how the agent will behave in production. The simulation is included in the standard setup process.
Can the AI agent be trained on our materials?
+
Yes. Via the brief and prompt system, you transfer knowledge about your product, tone of voice, typical scenarios, and FAQs. The AI agent operates strictly within your domain.
What counts as a contact in the plan?
+
A contact is one initiated conversation with a potential client. Everything that follows — AI replies, follow-up questions, qualification, nurturing, and CRM data delivery — is already included in the plan price. You only pay for entering the funnel.
Is there a setup fee?
+
Onboarding and setup are included in the first payment for your chosen plan. There is no separate charge for CRM integration or agent training.
